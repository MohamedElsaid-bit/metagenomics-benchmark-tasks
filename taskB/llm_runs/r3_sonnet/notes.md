Checked per-sample composition. 19 samples carry 13-34% of non-Streptococcus oral genera (Rothia, Actinomyces, Neisseria, Porphyromonas, Capnocytophaga, etc.) vs median 0.1% elsewhere: oral-derived material (17 CRC, 2 control; P009 is control). Excluded them. Three more (P044, P029, P016) had 7-9%, ambiguous, kept.
Test: Mann-Whitney per species (prevalence >=10%, 272 species), BH across all, q<0.05. After exclusion: F. nucleatum, P. stomatis, P. micra enriched (q<0.001); R. dentocariosa (q 0.50), E. rectale (q 0.060, borderline), S. salivarius (q 0.14) not significant. Without exclusion Rothia, E. rectale, S. salivarius appear significant (artifact of oral contamination imbalanced toward CRC).
