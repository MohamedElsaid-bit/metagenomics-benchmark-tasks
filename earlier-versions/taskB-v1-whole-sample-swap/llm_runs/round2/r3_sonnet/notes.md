PCA of log abundances showed 12 samples (all CRC) forming a distinct cluster dominated by oral taxa (Corynebacterium matruchotii, Neisseria, Atopobium, Porphyromonas, Prevotella oris/denticola, Actinomyces), i.e. mislabeled oral material, not stool. Excluded them.
Mann-Whitney U per species (prevalence >=10% in retained 114 samples; 267 species), BH FDR. With all samples Parvimonas, Gemella, Faecalibacterium appeared significant (artifact of oral contamination in CRC); after exclusion only F. nucleatum and P. stomatis remain at q<0.05 (Parvimonas q=0.085, Gemella q=0.14, Faecalibacterium q=0.50).
